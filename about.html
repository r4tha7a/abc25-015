About Us

Acacia Clinics provides convenient access to quality primary healthcare. The team embodies our brand promise “Excellent Care Always”, with warmth and professionalism for every client.
Founded in 2012, Acacia Clinics is 100% citizen-owned and managed by Dr. Gaamangwe Tanyala and Dr. Senate R. Tanyala, both passionate medical practitioners. For more than a decade, we have delivered primary healthcare services across Botswana, guided by our ethos of excellence in a clean, hospitable environment.
Our flagship clinic is strategically located at Unit 3, Plot 82158, Lejara Road, Broadhurst Industrial, Gaborone, just minutes from the city centre.
